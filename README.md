# Data-Analysts-Passengers-Load-Factors-
Load factor in the airport in one month. 
## Project Overview: This data analysis aims to provide a traffic in January, 2026 in airport. 
## Dataset: 
Dataset are in .csv format. ***Noted: Due to data privacy and governance, some aspect of it had been changed, however the key measurement had not in order to maintain the insightful in this analysis. 
## Tools
Only Microsoft Excel had been utilize to: 

1- Clean Data - Formular and Function, Power Query

2- Modeling - Power Pivot

3- Analysis and Dashboarding - Power Pivot.

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

1- What is the total passenger throughput for the month?

2- Is there a significant imbalance between arrivals (11,611) and departures (13,731)?

3- What does this imbalance imply about market demand or seasonality?

### Results/Finding: 

1- Strong outbound demand — likely driven by post-holiday return travel, labor migration, or business travel exodus.

2- Possible seasonal effect — January often sees outbound surges after New Year arrivals, indicating a seasonal skew rather than a structural issue.

3- Weaker inbound demand — may require targeted inbound marketing or route promotions to balance traffic flow in future months.
